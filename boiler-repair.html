Fast Diagnostic & Troubleshooting

Emergency Symptoms We Resolve Daily

Boilers operate under high pressure and intense heat. Calling an uncertified technician or attempting DIY repairs often damages expensive heat exchangers. Our team brings dedicated digital diagnostic instruments directly to your door.

No Heat or Frozen Hydronic Lines

Usually caused by seized circulator pumps, airlocks in hydronic radiators, burnt zone valve motors, or open low-water safety cutoffs.

Loud Kettling, Gurgling, or Banging Pipes

Mineral lime scale choke restricts water flow through cast iron sections, creating violent steam boiling. Solved via professional descaling and power flushes.

Relief Valve Leaking Water or Pressure Dropping to 0 PSI

Indicates waterlogged diaphragm expansion tanks, defective backflow PRVs, or internal heat exchanger seal failures.

Standardized Diagnostic Protocol

Our 5-Step Boiler Repair Methodology

Every service call follows our strict engineering guidelines to guarantee safety and prevent repeat breakdowns.

01

Hydrostatic Pressure Check

We test static vs. operating pressure (12-15 PSI for hot water, 0.5-2 PSI for steam systems) to verify bladder health.

02

Combustion & Flame Analysis

Digital flue gas measurement to check CO parts-per-million, draft integrity, and burner flame cleanliness.

03

Safety Cutoff & Aquastat Limits

Testing Low Water Cut Off (LWCO) probe switches, high-temperature limits, and spill switches.

04

Circulator & Zone Valve Testing

Verifying motor amp draw, relay boards, and end-switch contacts to ensure all heat zones receive flow.

05

Chemical Deaeration & Flush

Removing trapped oxygen and mineral sediment to restore quiet, frictionless heat delivery across your radiators.
