City of Joliet & Will County Compliance

Our 5-Step Commercial Compliance Protocol

We ensure your commercial facility remains fully certified under Illinois state fire marshal and municipal safety codes.

01

State Safety Inspections

Pre-inspection audits and hydrostatic pressure checks to guarantee high and low-pressure boilers pass municipal certification.

02

Low Water Cutoff Testing

Certified slow-drain and quick-drain blowdown testing recorded directly into your permanent facility logbook.

03

Flame Safeguard & Limits

Calibration of combustion flame scanners, high-limit aquastats, and automatic gas shutoff valves per CSD-1 code.

04

Backflow & Chemical Log

Annual RPZ backflow preventer certification and hydronic water chemistry pH balance to prevent boiler tube scale.

05

Guaranteed 2-Hour SLA

Contracted commercial accounts enjoy guaranteed 2-hour technician arrival during freezing winter conditions.

Zero Violation Guarantee
